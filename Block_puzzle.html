Privacy Policy

Block Puzzle

Effective date: 19 September 2026

Developer: Gideon Kipngetich · vaultiq.apps@gmail.com

Block Puzzle is a single-player puzzle game. It does not ask for your name, your email address, or any account. It has no sign-in, no social features and no server of its own. This policy explains the small amount of data that is involved anyway, and who handles it.

In short: we do not collect, transmit or store any personal information about you. Your scores and progress stay on your device. The only data that leaves your phone is what the advertising service collects in order to show ads, described below.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves your progress locally, using Android's standard app storage:

  • High scores, player level, experience points and lifetime statistics
  • Coin balance, boosters, unlocked themes and unlocked achievements
  • Daily mission progress and your daily reward streak
  • Settings — sound, music, haptics, theme and accessibility preferences
  • Whether you have purchased the ad-free upgrade

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it and it is not transmitted anywhere. It is removed when you uninstall the game. If you have Android's automatic backup enabled, your device may include this data in your own Google backup — that backup is controlled entirely by your Google account settings, not by us.

Advertising

The free version of the game shows advertisements supplied by Meta Audience Network. Meta acts as an independent data controller for the information it collects in order to serve and measure those ads, which typically includes your device's advertising identifier, coarse device and app information, and ad interaction events.

We do not receive, store or have access to this information. How Meta uses it is governed by Meta's own policies:

  • Meta Privacy Policy
  • Meta Audience Network data policy

You can limit or reset ad personalisation at any time on your device under Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can also delete your advertising ID entirely. Purchasing the ad-free upgrade stops advertisements being shown.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled by Google and are never seen by, sent to, or stored by this game or its developer. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase completed, which the game uses to grant the item. Google's handling of that transaction is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

The game records gameplay events — such as a game starting or ending, or a reward being claimed — to understand how the game is played and to balance its difficulty and economy. These events describe gameplay only. They contain no personal information, no contact details and nothing that identifies you personally.

Children

The game is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the game shows advertising supplied by a third party, parents may wish to use the advertising ID controls described above, or the family controls available in Google Play.

Permissions the app requests, and why

  • VIBRATE — haptic feedback when placing pieces and clearing lines. You can turn this off in the game's settings.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — used only by the advertising and billing services above. The game itself has no network features and plays fully offline.
  • AD_ID — allows the advertising service to read your device's advertising identifier, as described under Advertising.

Your rights and choices

  • Reset everything: uninstalling the game removes all locally stored progress and settings.
  • Limit ad tracking: use your device's advertising ID controls, described above.
  • Remove ads: purchase the ad-free upgrade in the game's store.

Because we hold no personal data about you on any system we control, there is no account to close and nothing for us to export or erase on request. Requests relating to advertising data should be directed to Meta, and requests relating to payments to Google, using the links above.

Changes to this policy

If the game's handling of data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date at the top revised. Material changes will also be reflected in the app's Google Play listing.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about the game can be sent to vaultiq.apps@gmail.com.
